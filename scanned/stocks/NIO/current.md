## What NIO does

NIO designs and sells premium electric vehicles in China (ES/EC/ET sedan + SUV lineup, plus the Onvo and Firefly sub-brands targeting mass-market and Europe). Differentiator is the battery-swap network — fast battery exchange in lieu of charging. FY2025 revenue ~$8B (rev/sh $3.50), losing ~$3B on a cash-burn-heavy cost structure with low gross margins (8.4%). 1.11M cumulative deliveries crossed end of April. Cash $31.3B (mostly customer/regulatory cash plus reserves), debt low. Listed as ADR on NYSE since 2018.
