## What CRCL does

Circle issues USDC, the second-largest US-dollar stablecoin. Primary revenue is the spread on T-bill yield × USDC reserves. It also runs Arc (a stablecoin-focused L1 blockchain), Circle Mint (on/off-ramp), and Circle Payments Network (settlement). FY2025 revenue $2.75B, net loss $69.5M. IPO 2025-06-05 at $31; recent ATH $298.99.
