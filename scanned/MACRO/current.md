## What's driving each pillar

**Growth ↑.** GDPNow nowcasting 3.7% for Q2 is well above stall speed. Payrolls still adding (organic ~+100K stripping the strike reversal), unemployment stable at 4.3%, well below the SAHM-rule recession trigger. Small-cap leadership confirms the reflationary read at the tape level.

**Inflation ↑.** Headline CPI at 3.3% is 130 bps above the Fed's target, with energy doing the bulk of the work. Core at 2.6% is sticky on services. Gold at all-time highs and oil at $103 corroborate — markets are paying for inflation hedges.

**Liquidity: tight, with cracks.** Fed funds at 3.50–3.75% is restrictive. The 8-4 vote at the April meeting shows real dissent: one member pushed to cut, three pushed back against the easing-bias language. Powell stays through any DOJ investigation — no dovish chair-swap bid coming.

## How to use this read

- **Direction bias:** long cyclicals + small caps + energy/materials; short or underweight long bonds + defensives. Tactical hedges via VIX (16 = cheap calls into prints).
- **Sizing:** in-regime longs (small caps, energy, financials) full size. Out-of-regime longs (long-duration tech, long bonds) half size.
- **Pacing:** add into pullbacks while VIX < 20 and the curve stays positive. Trim if VIX > 22 and RUT loses its 50-day.
